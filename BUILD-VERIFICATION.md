# Lume Gallery 14.0.0 — verification

## Scope and results

PhotoStudio now uses copied recipes for pixel adjustments, geometry, normalized drawing/text, bounded undo/redo and copy export. VideoStudio uses Media3 ExoPlayer for playback and Transformer for actual exports. A shared VideoRecipe builds crop, color, caption and output sizing. Export also applies source clipping, speed, pitch, gain/fades and concatenated clips. M4A output removes the video track. Photo and video tools remain ordinary gallery windows; vault isolation is unchanged.

## Limits and device checks

No physical-device playback, hardware encoding, MediaStore publishing or model-inference run is claimed. On OnePlus, verify H.264/H.265 and HDR-to-SDR output, AAC sync at multiple speeds, aspect/caption placement, mixed-rotation concatenation, silence handling, M4A, cancellation and each installed AI tool. Hardware may fall back to a supported size/codec. Preview is SDR playback; it is not a color-managed proof of HDR output. Video AI is current-frame processing and static face framing only, without temporal tracking or learned whole-video restoration/removal. Photo preview is sampled; normal output is capped at 4096 on the long side, and AI output is limited to the model result. Noise smoothing and glare reduction are pixel effects, not learned restoration of lost detail. Object repair opens a separate working-copy editor. Existing privacy/network disclosures remain in PRIVACY.md.
